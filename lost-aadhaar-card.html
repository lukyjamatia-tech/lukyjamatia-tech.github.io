Aadhaar Card Kho Gaya — Ab Kya Karein?

Aadhaar kho jana pareshani ki baat hai, lekin ise theek karna mushkil nahi. Neeche teen cheezein hain: turant kya karna hai, duplicate kaise milega, aur police report kab zaroori hai.

Sabse pehle ye jaan lein: Aadhaar number kho jane se koi aapka bank account nahi khol sakta. Har jagah biometric ya OTP verification lagta hai. Ghabrane ki zaroorat nahi — bas process follow karein.

1. Duplicate Aadhaar kaise download karein

Agar aapka registered mobile number aapke paas hai, to naya card 5 minute mein mil jayega:

  1. myAadhaar portal (myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) kholein
  2. Aadhaar number ya Enrolment ID daalein
  3. Registered mobile par OTP aayega — usse verify karein
  4. e-Aadhaar PDF download ho jayega
  5. PDF ka password: naam ke pehle 4 letters (CAPITAL) + janm ka saal. Jaise RAMESH, 1990 → RAME1990

Ye e-Aadhaar har jagah utna hi valid hai jitna original card.

Agar mobile number yaad nahi ya band ho gaya hai

Phir portal se download nahi hoga. Nazdeeki Aadhaar Seva Kendra jana padega, apne saath koi doosra pehchan patra le jaayein. Wahan biometric verification hoga aur number update karwa sakte hain.

2. Police mein report kab karani chahiye

Duplicate Aadhaar ke liye FIR zaroori nahi hai. Lekin in halaton mein report karana samajhdari hai:

  • Card wallet ya bag ke saath chori hua ho
  • Doosre documents (PAN, ATM, licence) bhi saath mein gaye hon
  • Kisi office, school ya bank ne proof of loss maanga ho
  • Aapko lagta ho ki koi galat istemal kar sakta hai

Zyadatar mamlon mein poori FIR nahi, sirf "lost report" ya General Diary entry hoti hai — wo aasan aur jaldi hoti hai.

3. Extra suraksha ke liye

  • Aadhaar lock karein — myAadhaar portal se biometric lock kar sakte hain. Zaroorat par khud hi unlock kar lein.
  • Authentication history dekhein — pichhle 6 mahine mein aapka Aadhaar kahan-kahan use hua, portal par dikh jata hai. Koi anjaan entry mile to UIDAI ko report karein.
  • Virtual ID (VID) use karein — asli Aadhaar number dene ki jagah 16-digit VID de sakte hain.

Agar kisi ko aapka card mila to?

Bahut se documents isliye wapas nahi milte kyunki jisko mila usse pata hi nahi hota ki kya kare. Bharatiya kanoon mein cheez milne wale par asli malik ko dhoondhne ki zimmedari hoti hai.

Aadhaar kho jane par FIR zaroori hai?

Duplicate download karne ke liye nahi. Misuse ka dar ho ya kahin proof maanga jaye, tab lost report karani chahiye.

Duplicate Aadhaar ka kitna kharcha hai?

myAadhaar se e-Aadhaar download karna free hai. Seva Kendra se PVC card mangwane par mamuli shulk lagta hai.

Kitne din mein mil jata hai?

Online download turant. PVC card post se aane mein aam taur par 7–15 din lagte hain.

Bina mobile number ke kya ho sakta hai?

Online nahi. Aadhaar Seva Kendra jana hoga jahan biometric se pehchan hogi.
